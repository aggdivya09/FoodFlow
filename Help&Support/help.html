Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions.

How can I track my order?

1. Go to My Orders from the menu.

2. Select the order you want to track.

3. Click Track Order to view the latest status.

How do I cancel an order?
Go to My Orders, select your order and choose the Cancel Order option, if cancellation is available.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards and other available payment options.
How can I request a refund?
Contact our support team with your order details to request a refund.
